# Room 403 TV surveillance watcher

Room403TVWatcher owns observation stages and the TV-only player/figure proxies. InspectableCaseTelevision retains the existing screen, power/inspection flags, procedural startup static, electronics audio and RenderTexture/material ownership. Room403HorrorDirector retains other room events, but suppresses its real-room doorway shadow and TV-triggered global flicker when a TV watcher is installed.

The existing auxiliary camera is repositioned at the actual room's high northwest corner. It manually renders 320x180 at approximately 13fps, only while the player is in the room. Its normal enabled flag remains false. A named Room403TVOnly layer isolates the faceless player coat and watcher silhouettes; all other scene cameras exclude it. Their renderers cast no shadows and have no colliders, so they cannot affect main-view visibility or movement. The main player camera and existing retro rendering are retained. The auxiliary camera has no RetroCameraStyle, disables post-processing/HDR/MSAA and uses a lightweight screen-only CCTV material pass.

The TV player proxy follows sampled actual position and horizontal view direction with approximately 0.3s delay. Stages are Normal → Doorway (far corner beside the bathroom) → Approaching (about 2m behind the last TV-facing pose, adjusted onto the floor strip to avoid the bed) → Behind (about 0.65m behind) → Finished. A stage must be watched for at least 0.8s, followed by at least 6s looking away before a new figure position appears. Looking at the TV holds its position. Looking back from the final stage for 1s hides it and briefly blends static only on the TV image. A quiet 0.35s spatial CRT static sound originates at the television; there is no figure-entry stinger, face, attack, chase, full-screen flash or forced camera movement.

TVWatcherFinished is a per-case saved cosmetic completion flag. The existing KeyTagVisible environment rule requires TelevisionInspected and TVWatcherFinished; the watcher completes after at most 40s following inspection even if observation stages are skipped. Previously visible keys or found key evidence complete the watcher immediately. Saved completion does not replay the figure sequence. Existing KeyEvidenceFound, receipt rewriting/reinspection and case completion remain unchanged.

The authoring menu **Archive 03:17 / Add Room 403 TV Watcher** applies the scene and layer changes through Unity Editor APIs. The room interior builder invokes it for new builds. RenderTextures are released when the TV is disabled and destroyed with its runtime materials on destruction; proxy sampling is bounded and no per-frame object search is added.

The actual Play Mode regression records native TV frames and a main-view look-back screenshot under `TVWatcher/`. It exercises the normal feed, player proxy, isolated layer masks, no physical old shadow, fallback and saved completion restoration, all observation stages, unchanged position while watched, final close figure, empty real room, quiet spatial static, key release and all existing CASE 001 evidence/completion checks. Fallback is checked using an accelerated 41-second Tick in actual Play Mode; completed-flag restoration is checked by reinitialization. The full case suite also tests actual scene reload after room completion. Mid-watcher application restart, physical keyboard/mouse, audible balance, monitor brightness and natural human observation timing remain manual checks. Screenshot readability is inspected separately from automated assertions.
